# SensaShop (Shopify Clone)

## What is this product?
SensaShop is a full-featured, modern e-commerce storefront and merchant admin dashboard engineered to replicate and match the exact Shopify Polaris design system, user experience, and aesthetic.

## Target Audience
Merchants, store managers, and e-commerce operators who require an intuitive, fast, and high-performance e-commerce platform with enterprise-grade Shopify Polaris UX.

## Core Commitments & Constraints
- Strict adherence to the official Shopify Brand Identity & Polaris design system.
- Authentic Shopify Color Palette:
  - **Verde Característico (Signature Green):** `#96bf48`
  - **Shopify Blue (Azul):** `#479ccf`
  - **Polaris Admin Action Green:** `#008060`
  - **Cinza Escuro / Obsidian (Dark Slate):** `#2d3538` & `#1a1a1a`
  - **Fundos Neutros (Canvas / Light Gray):** `#f5f5f5` & `#f2f7fa`
  - **Texto / Inks:** `#202223`, `#666666`, `#6d7175`, `#8c9196`
- Typography: Clean sans-serif with high readability, balanced weight steps, and `tabular-nums` for monetary values.
- Elevation & Components: Polaris Cards with 12px-16px radius, subtle 1px border and soft shadows, interactive state micro-animations.
